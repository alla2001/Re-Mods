Development Process

Our weapon development follows a comprehensive workflow:

  1. Consultation: Discuss your weapon concept, reference materials, and requirements
  2. Research: Gather technical specifications, visual references, and performance data
  3. 3D Modeling: Create high-quality 3D model with proper topology and UV mapping
  4. Texturing: Apply PBR textures with realistic materials and wear patterns
  5. Animation: Develop smooth, realistic animations for all weapon actions
  6. Scripting: Implement weapon behavior, ballistics, and game mechanics
  7. Sound Integration: Add authentic weapon sounds and audio effects
  8. Testing: Rigorous multiplayer and singleplayer testing for bugs and balance
  9. Delivery: Final mod package with documentation and installation guide
